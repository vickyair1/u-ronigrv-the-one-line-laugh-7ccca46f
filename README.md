One funny line for [The One-Line Laugh](https://make.fun/competitions/u-ronigrv-the-one-line-laugh-7ccca46f) on make.fun — a single sentence of at most 200 characters, any topic, judged by a house judge that keeps the bigger laugh.

## The line

> My smart fridge emailed me a diet plan, then ordered pizza behind my back and blamed the Wi-Fi.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (and prints it to stdout). Nothing else — no network, no input, fully deterministic.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
